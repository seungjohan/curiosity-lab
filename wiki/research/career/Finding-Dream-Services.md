# Research: Services for Discovering Purpose & "Dreams"

This research synthesizes the current landscape of platforms designed to help individuals discover professional or personal purpose.

## 1. Categorization of Approaches

| Approach | Focus | Examples | Pros/Cons |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Framework-based** | Self-assessment quizzes (IKIGAI, Gallup Strengths) | Gallup CliftonStrengths, MBTI-derived tools | Pro: Structural consistency. Con: Static, lacks actionable iteration. |
| **AI-Guided (Socratic)** | Interactive conversational coaching | AI coaches, specialized LLM wrappers | Pro: Dynamic, personalized inquiry. Con: Requires high-quality prompting to avoid bias. |
| **Cohort-based/Action** | Learning by doing with peers | Reforge, specialized niche accelerators | Pro: High accountability. Con: High barrier to entry (cost/time). |
| **Psychometric/Behavioral** | Data-driven talent matching | Pymetrics, high-end recruiters | Pro: Objectivity. Con: Often narrow (hiring focus only). |

## 2. Emerging Trends
*   **From "Questionnaire" to "Agent":** Instead of answering 100 questions, AI-powered systems are leveraging conversational agents to uncover subconscious patterns over weeks of interaction.
*   **The "Shadow" Discovery:** Platforms now focus on identifying *why* someone is stuck (e.g., cognitive offloading, impostor syndrome) before attempting to identify "what" the dream is.
*   **Integration with Action:** The most effective platforms no longer just identify the "dream"—they force immediate testing of the hypothesis via micro-projects.

## 3. Potential Ideation Wedges
*   **Personal Purpose Graph:** Using the framework of [[../ideation/The Connection|The Connection]] to map personal interests *as* a graph to identify "emergent" purpose, rather than linear career progression.
*   **AI-Facilitated "Micro-Trial":** An agentic service that helps users design and execute 48-hour "tests" of a potential career path.
