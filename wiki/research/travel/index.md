> [!IMPORTANT] Key Takeaway
> **Why this matters:** Travel research here is not general tourism — it is the logistics layer around a **culinary voyage**: turning the restaurant database into an itinerary that is bookable, walkable, and affordable before the trip starts.
> **How to use it:** Run [[guidelines|Voyage Guidelines]] as the pre-trip checklist, keep candidate destinations in [[spot|Travel Spots]], and reach for [[vocabulary|Travel Vocabulary]] when a booking or a local term is unfamiliar.
> **Informs:** Trip planning against [[../cooking/selected-restaurants|Selected Restaurants]], and the itinerary side of [[../../projects/Michelin Filter|Michelin Filter]].

# ✈️ Travel Research: Master Index

The hub for destination candidates, voyage logistics, and the vocabulary that travels with them.

## 📂 Categories
- [[guidelines|Voyage Guidelines & Checklist]] — the standard pre-trip process: data refresh, the 3-star hunt, advance booking, proximity grouping, and the universal 1–4 price tier mapping.
- [[spot|Travel Spots]] — running list of destinations and sites worth going to.
- [[vocabulary|Travel Vocabulary]] — accommodation, transport, and regional terms across languages.

## 🔗 Connections
