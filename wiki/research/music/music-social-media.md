# 🎼 Music-Focused Social Media Research

While there is no direct "Instagram of Music" that dominates the market, several platforms address segments of this need.

## 📱 Existing Platforms

| Platform | Core Focus | Social Mechanic | Instagram Comparison |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **BandLab** | Collaboration | Collaborative creation, community feed | High (Feed, Profile, Social) |
| **Vampr** | Networking | Professional connection (LinkedIn-like) | Low (Networking focus) |
| **SoundCloud** | Distribution | Commenting on waveforms, reposts | Medium (Feed, but UI is audio-focused) |
| **Resso** | Consumption | Lyrics-based interaction, shared music | High (Very social/visual) |

## 💡 Why an "Instagram for Music" is Difficult
1.  **Context**: Music is inherently auditory and temporal; Instagram is visual and static (or short-form video).
2.  **Discovery vs. Creation**: Balancing a "viewer" feed (consumption) with "creator" tools (uploading/mixing) is technically complex.
3.  **Copyright**: Licensing music for social sharing is a massive hurdle.
