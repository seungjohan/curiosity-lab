# Show Your Way — Instagram for Personal Methods

*(Working title.)*

## The Idea

After the mobile market opened up, a lot of new personal spaces appeared on the phone. Each one is private, and each one is done differently by every person:

| Space | "The way" worth seeing |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Home screen** | How someone lays out and organizes their screen |
| **Installed apps** | What they chose to keep, and what they deleted |
| **Phone cleanup** | How they tidy and list things |
| **AI usage** | Their prompts, their workflows, how they think with AI |
| *(more)* | Any personal digital habit |

Instagram shares the lifestyle through photos. This would share **the ways**, and they can be insightful or impressive to other people. I'm personally curious how other people build their phone screen and how they use AI.

## Collection vs. Method

- [[Private Space - The Bedroom Moved Online]] is about the **collection**: the room itself, starting with playlists. The motive is intimacy.
- This note is about the **method**: how someone does it and why. The motive is curiosity and learning ("I'm going to try that").

Existing signals: "What's on my iPhone" and home-screen tour videos, prompt-sharing threads. The demand exists, but it's scattered across other platforms and has no home of its own.

## Not Only a Social Platform

After watching the [5,300명 중 단 1명, 기업이 고른 AI 인재는 누구였을까 | AX 인재전쟁 본선 풀영상 — 조코딩 JoCoding](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1eyDhaKx--w) video, I realized the need doesn't have to be met by a social app. **The need is "I want to see how other people do it." Social media is only one delivery format.**

| Format | How "the way" gets shown | What it adds |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Social feed** (the original framing) | Async posts, screenshots, write-ups | Scale, always-on browsing |
| **Competition / hackathon** (the video) | People use AI live, under constraints, in front of judges | Live proof: you can't fake a method in real time; companies can hire from it |
| **Conference / talk / meetup** | Someone walks through their setup and reasoning | Depth; the "why" behind the method |
| *(others)* | Workshops, livestreams, curated collections | — |

The event formats show something a feed can't: **the method in action, not a finished snapshot.** That may answer the open question below about capturing a *way* rather than a *snapshot*.

## Open Questions

- Do viewers want **"so that's who they are"** (intimacy) or **"I'll copy that"** (method)?
- Which method is first, the way the playlist was for Private Space? Home screen (visual, one screenshot) vs. AI prompts (high value, higher privacy risk).
- How do you capture a *way* instead of only a *snapshot*?
