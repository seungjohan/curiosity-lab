# Abundance flips value

When something floods to abundant and near-free, its *opposite* becomes the scarce, premium, meaningful thing. Value migrates to whatever stays rare.

**Test:** does the page bet that a thing becoming cheap/infinite makes its counterpart (physical, human, interpretive) the new premium?

**Polarity (`axis: bits-vs-atoms`):** pages sit on the `atoms` side (physical grounding as premium) or the `bits` side (more digital abstraction) — opposing sides are candidate tensions.
