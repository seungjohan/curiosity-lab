# Abstraction raises altitude

When a tool abstracts away the low-level work, human attention and value move *up* to higher-level intent — you stop writing syntax and start setting direction.

**Test:** does the page describe focus shifting from *how* (mechanics) to *what/why* (intent) because a layer got automated?

**Double edge (`axis: leverage-vs-atrophy`):** the same shift that frees an expert to work at higher altitude can let a novice *offload the underlying skill entirely* and never build it. Pages on the `leverage` side treat this as empowerment; pages on the `atrophy` side treat it as a risk (e.g. cognitive offloading in education). Opposing sides are candidate tensions.
