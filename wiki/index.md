# Index

A personal vault for researching whatever I'm curious about — the AI industry and job market, cooking, music, language, planting, endurance sport — and letting that research turn into ideas, projects, and writing.

Everything moves through one loop:

**Capture → Research → Synthesize → Ideate → Build → Reflect ↺**

Research accumulates as notes. [Concepts](concepts/index.md) are where notes from different categories meet and become ideas. Ideas that earn it graduate to projects. What I learn building flows back into the research. See [../LINKING.md](../LINKING.md) for how pages connect, and [../AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) for the full working manual.
