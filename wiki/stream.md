# Stream

Messy thoughts, newest first. Random thought, diary, idea, blame, question, nothing in particular — all of it belongs.

Write a heading and dump underneath. Don't tidy it, don't finish it, don't decide if it's good:

```
## 2026-07-17 wine lists are a rip-off

whatever, however long, real line breaks, pipes fine
```

That's your whole job. **I add the kind emoji, the status, and the `Connects:` line** — say *"connect pass"* whenever. Use the **Outline panel** (right sidebar) to scan every entry at a glance.

**Kind:** 💭 random · 📔 diary · 💡 idea · 😤 blame · ❓ question
**Status:** 🌱 raw · 🔗 connected · 🎓 graduated · 🪦 dropped — nothing is ever deleted.
